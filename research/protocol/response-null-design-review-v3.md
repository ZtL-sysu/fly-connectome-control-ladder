# Candidate E design repair v2: independent targeted design review

## Verdict

**The design repair is directionally coherent, and its stage ordering is mostly correct.** It separates structural support that can be established statically from empirical response/geometry common support and output-margin calibration that require the post-guidance development pilot. The startup packet may require an executable construction, static legality/support evidence, and resource plausibility; it must not require a successful response-match, an empirical `delta_noise`, or any scientific outcome before guidance.

The proposed evoked-dose / absolute-response distinction is scientifically sensible, as are the revised continuous-only E2 claim, explicit ReLU-zero convention, uncertainty propagation, and multiplicity family. Two material design items remain before startup can be accepted: (1) specify how a **composed mask's aggregate current forcing** enters construction/scoring, not just per-source/per-swap vector distances; (2) resolve whether the calibration is a noise-only perturbation or a full augmentation package, using the verified source semantics below. Static support and measured resource artifacts are still pending; no empirical gate is passed by this review.

## Targeted assessment

### Evoked dose versus absolute matching: coherent roles, one missing link

Using baseline-subtracted `beta_s` to define an intervention dose avoids counting a high-DC source as evidence that many task-evoked edges changed. Separately matching **absolute** ReLU responses across gray and development trajectories correctly controls DC forcing as well as evoked changes. These are different diagnostics and do not conflict. The legal group-key and exact shared-parameter constraints, edge-carried payload rule, simple-graph/parallel-edge fail-closed behavior, and unweighted topology certificate are appropriate.

**Remaining construction blocker:** the design describes response matching as a weight-scaled squared difference of source-response vectors, but it does not explicitly say that a *completed mask* is accepted/scored by its aggregate forcing over targets. For each composed mask, calculate and retain `r[t] = (A_null-A_original) ReLU(v_original[t])` for the absolute gray and development trajectories, with a frozen clip/time/node aggregation. Pairwise swap scores can cancel or reinforce at a shared target; therefore a sum of individually favorable distances is not the realized mask forcing and cannot stand in for it. Use per-swap scores only for proposal ranking, then compute aggregate forcing after every complete mask and use that value in the response-match acceptance/reporting rule. Keep `beta_s`/evoked dose as its separate nontrivial-edit measure.

Also define the gray stimulus concretely (input tensor/value, duration, and initial state) and how the gray trajectory is aligned with each clip before baseline subtraction. Report the fraction of total and eligible edge mass with `beta_s=0` or unstable. This matters because a nominal dose can still be concentrated in a small subset of source types or masks even if its whole-graph denominator is nonzero.

### Noise and uncertainty: correction is implementable, but not source-equivalent by default

The source audit in `code-audit/sensory-noise-semantics-v1.md` confirms:

- Code uses `.08` as the standard deviation of additive Gaussian noise on normalized grayscale inputs (`sintel_utils.py:48-58`; `augmentation/hex.py:290-322`). The paper XML `Sec18/Par60` calls `sigma_n=.08` a variance; preserve that reporting discrepancy and follow the executable code for replication.
- The code clamps below at zero and does not clamp above one. Noise is inserted after temporal crop but before contrast/brightness jitter and nearest-exact temporal input resampling (`datasets/sintel.py:578-587`). In the archived task metadata (`_meta.yaml:71-83`), 19 source frames at 24 Hz become 40 input samples at 50 Hz. Noise is sampled on the source-frame tensor, so resampling repeats some frame noise across adjacent integration samples.
- The noise transform has no noise seed or repeat count in the checkpoint config; task `seed: 0` does not identify noise draws. The verified archive contains 50 models with identical dataset-config hash, but this establishes the configured augmentation, not the exact draws or exposure count.

The design should choose and name one of two calibrands before the pilot:

1. **Noise-only operational reference:** isolate σ=.08 noise by disabling contrast, brightness, and geometric stochastic transforms; retain normalized input, lower-only clamp, crop and pre-resampling insertion, and the recorded resampling. Call this a source-inspired noise-only sensitivity scale, not the full training augmentation distribution.
2. **Joint-augmentation sensitivity:** retain the source's subsequent contrast/brightness and geometric transforms. Call the resulting scale joint augmentation sensitivity, not `delta_noise`, because output divergence cannot be attributed to Gaussian noise alone.

For either choice, pair clean and perturbed inputs by checkpoint, scene, initial state, decoder, temporal crop, and all non-noise transforms; freeze explicit noise tensors/seeds and repeat count on development data; propagate noise-draw and scene uncertainty through `delta_noise`. Do not set `delta_noise=.08`, and do not call it an intrinsic noise floor, perceptual bound, or biological equivalence tolerance. The uncertainty bootstrap described in design v2 is coherent if every replicate recomputes calibration and confirmation quantities while maintaining these pairs and resampling noise-draw/scene and mask-family clusters. With a finite fixed checkpoint panel, the estimand remains conditional on those trained solutions.

### E2 scope, recurrence, and multiplicity

The v2 change from a threshold-classification promise to a **continuous discrepancy forecast** is coherent. `MAE/delta_noise <= 0.5` can be stated as a scale-relative resolution target for the mean forecast error; it does not imply individual-case accuracy or reliable classification around `q=delta_noise`. Keeping threshold confusion results descriptive avoids that overclaim.

The fixed reference-gated recurrence and exact-zero convention `G=1[v>0]` are internally consistent with the linearization. Reporting near-zero and gate-crossing diagnostics after forecasts are sealed is appropriate and avoids tuning to confirmation. Preserve the restriction that original query trajectories are allowed only after masks are frozen and their forward cost is counted; actual null query trajectories/outputs cannot enter forecasts. For transferred masks, instantiate `A`/`A'` with each receiver's frozen parameters and identical original/null state/input pairs.

The finite Bonferroni family over directional E1 contrasts and the half-margin E2 gate is defensible if `K`, one- versus two-sided claims, cluster units, and bootstrap implementation are all frozen before confirmation. For primary comparator gains, the plan should identify whether every comparator is a gate or one is primary with others secondary. Resample complete mask families (not correlated masks from one swap chain as independent) alongside scenes and calibration-noise draws. Development-based simulation of the planned bootstrap is a precision-planning step; no confirmation-based removal of failed contrasts.

Pilot development signal is not a final result. |
| **Confirmation after pilot freeze** | Sealed predictions then separate null runs on predeclared scenes/models/mask families; finite family-wise inference. | The prospectively defined held-out estimands and stop/falsification outcomes. | Retraining, test-label accuracy from withheld Sintel labels, or post-hoc margin/dose changes. |

This separation resolves the sequencing concern **in principle**: design v2 explicitly says empirical common support/informative dose belongs to the pilot after guidance and says startup cannot claim it already passed. However, the “Remaining startup work” and implementation checklist need to distinguish static structural support from pilot-measured evoked support, and separate resource *plausibility* from scientific forward profiling. The former can be a startup artifact; the latter remains post-guidance pilot work. Any engineering dry run before startup should use constant inputs or timing-only workloads and no candidate responses/labels/outcomes, with its provenance clearly stated.

## Exact remaining design blockers before startup proceed

1. **Aggregate mask objective:** specify the complete-mask aggregate forcing calculation and how it enters acceptance/reporting. Do not let pairwise source-vector distance masquerade as aggregate current matching.
2. **Calibration estimand:** select noise-only versus full joint augmentation; document clamp, crop/noise/jitter/resampling order, baseline pairing, output normalization, draw manifest, and development-only uncertainty. Use the audited fact that archived `original_split` provenance is not established: model-000 `_meta.yaml` omits `original_split`; the `Task` constructor defaults it to false (`task/tasks.py:61`), while the current source task YAML sets it true and solver passes the composed config (`solver.py:203`). The paper says checkpoints use held-out Sintel validation clips and keep same-scene subsamples together (`flyvis2024-primary.xml:1`, `Sec18/Par57, Par61`), but that does not identify which split flag produced these exact archived checkpoints. Therefore do not treat a source validation partition or `seed:0` as a pristine confirmation/noise seed; freeze scene provenance and use genuinely disjoint confirmation inputs.
3. **Static support and cost artifact:** provide actual graph/config hashes, legal candidate counts and rejection breakdown, parameter-group compatibility across receivers, parallel-edge decision, and structural/topology-certificate reachability. Separately provide a no-label engineering or static compute/memory plausibility artifact and disclose decoder restore/version status. These startup artifacts are pending; this review does not mark them met.
4. **Pre-pilot protocol completeness:** specify exact gray baseline tensor/state, response vector/time/clip weighting, candidate proposal budget, geometry bins/tolerances and common-support decision procedure without filling empirical support. Freeze source/receiver/model/scene manifests and all failure rules. The permitted claim at startup is “plan executable subject to pilot gates,” not “response matching is feasible/effective.”
5. **Uncertainty family:** list exact primary endpoints and fixed `K`; define nested paired resampling over calibration scenes/noise draws, confirmation scenes, and mask families; keep model inference conditional on the fixed ensemble. Run development-only precision simulation only after pilot variance exists and before confirmation; if infeasible, revise design.

## Conditional recommendation

**Proceed with design-stage closure, not startup pass or experiment execution, until blockers 1–4 have an executable static specification and the pending static/resource artifacts are supplied.** The updated noise source audit satisfies the source-semantics lookup but does not satisfy `delta_noise` calibration. After guidance and research gates, the bounded P2 pilot may test empirical common support, estimate the operational margin, and derive variance/cost; its stop conditions must stay explicit. E1/E3 could carry the contribution if full-mask aggregate forcing supports meaningful response matching and confirmation later separates it from matched controls on untouched inputs/models. E2 may remain a continuous diagnostic under its half-margin gate, not a new algorithm claim. No results or empirical feasibility are asserted here.
