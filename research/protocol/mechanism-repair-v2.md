# Mechanism repair v2 — separate task projection from competence

Owner: ccf-idea-optimizer, standard mode. Existing D supplement and independent review remain preserved. This is a repair proposal, not a searched novelty verdict.

Raw weakness: “competent networks reveal topology effects” is neither necessary nor sufficient. A competent predictor can be invariant to a rewiring; an incompetent predictor can be highly sensitive. Input-shuffle sensitivity cannot establish power for small topology effects. More updates alone cannot carry the paper.

Revised bottleneck: behavioral null comparisons conflate three operations: altering recurrent computation, fitting a task readout, and adapting network parameters after rewiring. Distinguish these operations experimentally. Freeze recurrent features and fit identical train-only probes; freeze trained parameters/readout for immediate intervention; then compare after adaptation with identical budgets. Parameter sharing and edge-carried attributes are explicit parts of each estimand.

Mechanism: topology changes can move hidden trajectories in directions suppressed by the decoder, or change the alignment of output displacement with task residuals. Task information may be recoverable by a specified linear readout even when joint early optimization is weak. Hypothesis: a measurable, train/validation-derived task projection explains held-out intervention outcomes better than edge survival, degree statistics, activation magnitude or training step count. This hypothesis may fail and is not established by an exact loss identity alone.

Contribution type: mechanistic empirical finding plus predictive evaluation method if held-out evidence supports it. Not a novel positive-control checklist, graph rewiring algorithm, or algebraic theorem.

Falsifiers: no task information in frozen features; no difference between canonical and fitted readouts; projected response fails out-of-sample prediction; effect is fully explained by activity scale or endpoint metadata; only edge/bar tasks work; no independent seed/mask replication. Each ends or materially redirects this candidate.

Known priors: standard representation probing and perturbation/influence analyses may already cover the proposed evaluation method. Nature2024 already links visual tuning to connectivity; Structure Alone and FlyHack increase novelty risk. The focused literature screen must specifically test whether this exact question and empirical regime are already resolved. No high novelty score is claimed.
