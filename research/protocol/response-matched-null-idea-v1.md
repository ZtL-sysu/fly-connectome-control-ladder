# Candidate E — response-matched structural nulls

**Status:** prospective rescue candidate, not adopted, scored, novelty-cleared, or approved for experiments. It belongs to the existing task-aligned connectome-null family and is related to Candidate B in `idea-cards.md`; treat it as a concrete null-generation/evaluation branch of B unless later evidence establishes a distinct contribution.

## Normalized idea card

- **Task:** Determine what a fly visual connectome comparison can infer after degree-, sign-, cell-type-, and parameter-sharing-compatible graph controls are additionally matched to the frozen model’s local task responses on unlabeled development stimuli.
- **Problem/gap:** Standard graph nulls control structural statistics, but may change the recurrent current on the comparison model’s observed input trajectories so much that output differences conflate wiring with a large immediate functional perturbation. Recent task-aware/function-preserving connectome work makes this gap narrower: the question is not simply whether task information can guide graph editing, but whether label-free source-response-matched, parameter-preserving directed swaps produce materially different graphs whose frozen responses remain close on development inputs and whose mismatch predicts independent OOD divergence.
- **Root challenge:** Edge swaps alter recurrent computation, whose effect propagates through nonlinear, stateful dynamics. Similar source activity bounds the one-step current change only under strict edge-weight and parameter-sharing compatibility; aggregate residuals and later-time/OOD trajectories need separate measurement.
- **Core insight:** In the official default flyvis operator, if two legal edges (i\to j) and (k\to l) have identical effective weight (w), then swapping targets changes the original trajectory’s currents at (j,l) by (w(a_k-a_i)) and its negative, where (a=\mathrm{ReLU}(v)). Thus unlabeled development responses can propose swaps with small first-step forcing. For a whole rewired graph, compute the aggregate forcing (r_t=(A_{null}-A_{bio})a_t^{bio}); pairwise swap scores may cancel or reinforce. If aggregate forcing is exactly zero at every step from the same state, trajectory equality follows by induction for that input. Small forcing has no general long-horizon/OOD guarantee.
- **Mechanism and method:** Freeze one official, competent flyvis model, decoder, state initialization, input map, and all parameters. Generate degree-preserving directed double-edge swaps only among edges that preserve source/target cell-type compatibility, sign, exact effective weight, parameter-sharing identity, and all other model-specific restrictions. Reject self-loops, duplicate/forbidden edges, and any change to protected input/output mapping. Rank or accept legal swaps by source-activity-vector similarity computed only on unlabeled development stimuli. Recompute the *aggregate* forcing after composing swaps. Do not refit any weights or readout after rewiring. Generate structurally matched and geometry-matched controls with equal changed-edge budget, active-edge mass, accepted swap count, displacement distribution, and parameter-group composition as closely as feasible; also include activity-magnitude-only and shuffled response-to-neuron controls. Record residual imbalances rather than attribute effects to response matching when the controls cannot balance them.
- **Evaluation:** Predeclare source checkpoints, match/calibration stimuli, confirmation checkpoints, graph-mask identities, and scene/condition splits. Compare (a) response-matched nulls, (b) degree/sign/type/weight-sharing matched swaps without activity matching, (c) geometry-matched swaps, (d) activity-magnitude-only swaps, and (e) shuffled activity correspondence. Evaluate frozen output/trajectory difference and actual labelled-flow error separately. Test changed speeds/contrasts and scene-disjoint labelled Sintel development scenes. Original Sintel TEST imagery can test preservation of the reference model’s predictions only; its withheld flow labels cannot establish test accuracy. Include checkpoint identities not used during matching as a separate cross-model transfer estimand; do not pool it with per-model matching. Report uncertainty by independent source checkpoint and graph mask, not by video frames alone.
- **Topology validity check:** Edge-count change is not sufficient. Before interpreting a “different graph,” certify it is not a complete computational relabeling under fixed node, input, and output identities. Report edge overlap, reciprocal-pair count, weighted (\mathrm{tr}(A^2)) or equivalent cycle/motif measures, changed active-edge mass, and alignment to an allowed node permutation. A response-matched ensemble confined to silent/duplicate edges or graph automorphisms is not an informative topology control.
- **Contribution type:** Conditional-null construction plus a mechanistic empirical finding, if the topology changes substantially and response mismatch predicts out-of-sample consequences. Do not claim a new general rewiring algorithm, task accuracy method, or biological lesion/disconnection finding.

## Claims the experiment could support

A defensible result would show that, for this frozen model and a prespecified stimulus distribution, the degree/sign/weight-sharing-preserving graph ensemble contains substantially different topologies whose recurrent responses can be made indistinguishable within a calibrated development resolution, and then establish whether that preservation transfers to untouched conditions/model identities. A second result could show that development forcing predicts held-out divergence better than structural, geometry, and activity-scale baselines. If all controls preserve equally well, if generated graphs barely move, or if source-response matching gives no transfer, the candidate’s central claim fails.

The matching criterion conditions the graph distribution on response information from the frozen model. It is therefore a **conditional functional-equivalence ensemble**, not an unconditional degree-null distribution. Do not report an unconditional topology (p)-value or infer that the fly’s actual cells/edges are dispensable. The conclusion is about what structural evidence this model comparison can resolve under the stated condition.

## Assumptions and reviewer risks

- **Exact parameter semantics:** Need source-level verification for the official architecture/checkpoint that swapped edges really share an identical effective weight and all tied parameters remain tied. Matching numerically close weights is not enough for the exact local-current identity. Edge-carried versus endpoint-derived attributes must be fixed as part of the estimand.
- **Adequate frozen reference:** First establish that the frozen reference beats zero/constant and input-only/task-matched baselines on labelled development data. Response fidelity to a weak predictor is not competent task preservation. Keep this competency check distinct from response-based graph construction.
- **Enough nontrivial legal swaps:** Exact weight-group, sign, source/target type, graph constraints and geometry may leave few accepted swaps. If only near-neighbor or automorphism swaps are possible, this is a geometry or representation relabeling control, not a strong topology test.
- **Unlabelled does not mean independent:** The generation stimuli are label-free but still side information. Freeze them as development inputs, disclose their provenance, and keep all confirmation stimuli/output responses out of matching and hyperparameter selection.
- **Functional resolution:** Choose an equivalence margin before confirmation using development-only variation across competent source models or another prespecified calibration. Never pick a tolerance that makes generated nulls pass. The margin is an operational model-comparison scale, not a biological equivalence bound.
- **Attribution:** Feature-aware rewiring can exploit the frozen model’s computation. To claim the effect comes from response matching, compare against controls with matched swap dose, graph displacement, active weight and parameter groups. If complete balance is impossible, make a joint conditional-null claim and narrow causal attribution.
- **Venue fit:** CVPR needs a clear visual-mechanism result on genuinely held-out motion conditions/scenes. A graph-generation procedure alone or only synthetic single-edge results may be too narrow.

## Feasibility and resources

The parent reports that 50 official pretrained models/configurations are already present locally with official checksums verified. Their metadata identifies MultiTaskSintel fold 1, 19-frame sequences at (dt=.02), and DecoderGAVP. The official model paper/repository provides the flyvis code, pretrained models, and optic-flow setting. This enables an inference-only first screen and avoids a long retraining campaign. Validation sequences may have influenced checkpoint selection, so they are development/calibration data, not pristine confirmation.

A feasible first screen would use one predeclared checkpoint to compute source-response summaries on a small fixed set of unlabeled development sequences, build constrained swap proposals, and measure accepted non-isomorphic edge dose, aggregate forcing, development trajectory difference, and forward cost. Then freeze the generator before applying it to untouched model identities and stimuli. Cost is likely dominated by repeated temporal model inference and proposal evaluation; no timing or GPU-memory benchmark has been measured here. The existing prospective <=4 GPU-hour pilot ceiling in the mechanism note should remain the cap until a source-only device/timing audit shows actual costs.

For transfer evidence, use (1) synthetic moving-edge/bar stimuli with known flow and unseen speed/contrast combinations, (2) scene-disjoint labelled Sintel *development* examples with no overlap with the source fold, and (3) public Sintel TEST imagery only for agreement with the reference model, since TEST flow labels are withheld. Confirm the exact scene split/checkpoint-selection leakage before using any scene as independent. Report per-model matched nulls and cross-model transferred masks separately.

## Falsifiers and stop conditions

1. Legal swaps cannot achieve meaningful edge turnover after exact weight-sharing/sign/type constraints, or topology metrics show only computational isomorphisms.
2. Response matching does not improve development response preservation over dose- and geometry-matched structural controls.
3. Preservation depends on matching stimuli or identities and fails on held-out scenes/conditions; forcing does not predict held-out divergence beyond simple baselines.
4. All structural and response-matched controls are equivalent under adequately precise intervals, leaving no informative contrast.
5. Frozen reference competence is too weak to make output preservation task-relevant, or parameter-sharing semantics cannot be preserved exactly.

**Development label:** `needs-search` and `needs-feasibility-check`. Current novelty risk is high because recent connectome papers already examine function-preserving/task-aware structural changes; the narrowly specified conditional null and generalization estimand need primary-source distinction and eventual independent evidence before adoption.
