### Novelty delta

The plausible novelty is narrow: **a response-conditioned, no-refit ensemble of materially different Flyvis graphs, constructed by exact task-compatible equal-effective-weight directed swaps, with aggregate-current forcing measured on development trajectories and independent held-out function/transfer tests.** The source identity and exact forcing algebra support feasibility of that special construction, not novelty. If An, Zhou–Hasler, or Liu already implement the same fixed-weight response-conditioned intervention and held-out estimand, the current delta collapses to implementation detail and the idea must pivot or be abandoned as a distinct contribution. If their full methods do not, a result showing nontrivial active topology change and a held-out preservation boundary beyond matched structural, geometry, magnitude, and shuffled-response controls could make the narrow question venue-relevant.

## Normalized idea and mechanism judgment

- **Problem:** Ordinary structural nulls can alter the recurrent current so strongly that a downstream difference mixes topology with an immediate functional perturbation.
- **Gap:** Whether a conditional ensemble of degree/sign/type/parameter-sharing-compatible graphs can be selected from unlabeled responses of a frozen task model, yet still change active non-isomorphic topology and preserve frozen behavior beyond development inputs.
- **Mechanism:** For compatible edges with identical effective weight, swapping targets yields a paired local forcing proportional to source-activation difference. For composed masks, selection must use or report the aggregate (r_t=(A_{null}-A_{bio})\mathrm{ReLU}(v_{bio,t})). Exact zero forcing at every step from the same state/input gives exact trajectory equality by induction; approximate forcing alone gives no long-horizon/OOD guarantee.
- **Intervention/estimand:** Acute graph rewiring with trained recurrent parameters, decoder/readout, state initialization, input map, and node identities frozen. The response-conditioned graph family is a conditional null, not an unconditional degree-null sample and not evidence of biological dispensability.
- **Evidence path:** First establish competence of each frozen reference; then demonstrate a feasible common active-dose support for response-matched and strong controls, topology certificates, a calibrated operational margin, and held-out scene/condition/model evidence. Separate per-model matched masks from cross-model transferred masks. Naturalistic TEST imagery can assess teacher-function preservation only, not withheld-label flow accuracy.

The supplied source audit supports the restricted forcing identity under exact weight/group compatibility and identifies relevant source semantics. It does not establish a useful accepted-swap yield, common support, recurrent stability, non-isomorphism for any generated mask, or a positive result.

## Fatal risks versus fixable weaknesses

### Fatal to the central claim if confirmed

1. **Novelty collapse:** Full methods for one of the three closest preprints already contain the same frozen, no-refit activity-conditioned graph intervention and independent transfer evaluation. This is a pivot/stop condition for E as a distinct idea, not something a better title can repair.
2. **No informative common support:** Exact weight-group/type/sign constraints leave no nontrivial active-edge dose, or geometry/activity controls cannot overlap sufficiently to attribute preservation to response matching. Then the causal comparison is not identified at the intended dose; narrow to a joint conditional-null finding only if that remains meaningful.
3. **No topology change:** Accepted masks are isomorphic, silent/near-zero mass, or computational relabelings under the declared colors and fixed mappings. Edge turnover alone cannot rescue the claim.
4. **No independent preservation:** Equivalence disappears on held-out scenes/conditions or fails on the designated transfer arm, or all controls preserve equally well at adequately resolved common dose. This falsifies the proposed strong E1 result; do not move the margin after seeing confirmation outcomes.
5. **Invalid task interpretation:** The reference model is not competent against appropriate labelled development baselines, or input/output/readout mapping differs across compared pairs. Then prediction preservation is not task-relevant or is confounded.

## Independent reviewer panel

**Experiment expert.** Score tendency: 3/5. Smallest decisive gate: enumerate legal candidates/common support and profile a capped engineering workload before scientific inference. The critical comparison is response-matched versus common-dose geometry-matched and constrained-random controls under the frozen operational margin.

**AC / venue expert.** Score tendency: 3/5. CVPR can value a clear visual-computation result, but a null generator or local graph-equivalence result alone is likely too narrow. Require unseen visual conditions/scenes, credible model competence, and a finding that changes interpretation of structural evidence rather than only an algorithmic recipe. A planning-stage design is appropriate; a main-track claim is premature.

**Skeptical prior-art expert.** Score tendency: 2/5 until checked; this is a novelty uncertainty, not evidence of low novelty. The strongest rejection argument is that An already studies task-aware fly connectome rewiring with held-out task effects; Zhou–Hasler uses pretrained Flyvis with randomized graph/fidelity comparisons; Liu uses 50-member Flyvis null comparisons. The exact construction may remain distinct, but the supplied abstract-level screen cannot establish that. Full-method verification of these three is the highest-value next action.

**Panel synthesis.** Agreement: the fixed-weight source-current mechanism is technically plausible in its exact special case, and a bounded plan can test it without retraining. Decisive accept axis: a nontrivial, certified, response-conditioned null that preserves frozen outputs on independent inputs at a fixed, source-calibrated scale while matched controls do not. Decisive reject axis: close preprint overlap or no common nontrivial active-dose support. The panel therefore supports sequential guidance **planning under explicit gates**, not acceptance/full-paper development or execution.
