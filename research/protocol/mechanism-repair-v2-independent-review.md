## Decision

**Keep startup unresolved. Do not promote to full research-guidance planning or authorize a research run yet.** The new decomposition repairs an important conceptual flaw: competence is no longer treated as sufficient or necessary for topology sensitivity; frozen-feature probe, acute intervention and adaptation are distinct estimands. The exact loss identity is correctly demoted to algebra, not novelty. This makes a pilot more scientifically discriminative.

It does **not yet resolve the novelty gate**, and the task-projection predictor still needs a fully specified test-time feature map to rule out residual leakage. The strong-result contract properly calls the numeric lock, natural-workload access, task-level compute and strong-baseline feasibility unresolved. The allowed next step is focused prior-art screening and predictor/protocol specification. A bounded feasibility pilot can be reconsidered only after those gates; the proposed task queue is not scientific authorization by itself.

## What is materially improved

1. **Competence is an eligibility measure, not a causal proxy.** The repair explicitly recognizes that a competent model may be invariant to a given rewiring, while a weak model may be highly sensitive. This avoids the earlier invalid inference “near-.5 MSE ⇒ assay blind ⇒ topology effect may be hidden.”
2. **The assays answer different questions.** Canonical joint training measures optimization under the graph; ridge probes measure linear accessibility of each frozen feature set; acute rewiring with fixed weights/readout measures the immediate effect of changing recurrent computation; post-rewire adaptation measures recoverability under a specified training budget. A dissociation among these would be interpretable if their data splits, initializations and budgets are matched.
3. **The exact MSE identity is properly bounded.** For paired examples, same output coordinates, same frozen W,b and same MSE normalization, with `e = W h_bio + b − y` and `d = W(h_null − h_bio)`, the change is exactly `mean(2 e·d + ||d||²)`. It explains how projection and residual alignment can cancel latent displacement; it does not predict a new outcome without values for those terms and is not a theorem contribution.
4. **Estimand semantics are better controlled.** Keeping TopoSens’s disclosed edge-carried parameter assignment for the primary replication is correct; endpoint-consistent reassignment is a separate model/estimand, not a repair of a bug (`toposens2026.txt`, §4.2; `source-semantics-independent-audit.md`).
5. **The pilot is explicitly non-confirmatory.** The design says not to infer equivalence/generalization from S1/S2, reserves confirmation, and acknowledges third-party timings are not hardware results.

## Predictor and leakage audit

The formula in the matrix is algebraically valid **only for the acute frozen-readout comparison**: paired inputs, the same output dimensions/readout and loss normalization, with no adaptation between `h_bio` and `h_null`. It does not directly cover (a) different ridge probes fit separately per graph, (b) a readout retrained after rewiring, (c) network adaptation, or (d) different input samples. Those require separate outcome definitions.

The current text can support a **train/validation-fitted predictor** without test leakage, but only if the following distinction is enforced:

- Predictor fitting may use labels/residuals from its designated training/calibration data. It may use frozen W and observed paired displacements on those data to learn a mapping from pre-test observable features to intervention effects.
- For each held-out mask/task example, predictor inputs must be computable without `y_test`, `e_test`, test MSE, or model selection informed by test outcomes. If the proposed predictor literally evaluates `2 e_test·d_test + ||d_test||²`, it is the exact test outcome decomposition, not a prediction.
- The true held-out paired ΔMSE may be calculated **after predictions are frozen** and used only as the target for scoring predictive error/rank and uncertainty. Do not call this “prediction” if the labels were used to create its input.
- Prevent validation reuse: W fit on training data; predictor calibration and hyperparameter selection should use nested/cross-fitted validation or separate calibration and validation partitions. Then reserve fresh masks and independent motion identities for confirmation. Group splits by mask and task identity; stimulus frames from one movie are not independent network examples.
- Compare against prespecified predictors: edge overlap/survival, degree/geometry, total latent displacement, activity scale, constant/random. Include a train/validation-only simple predictor. Report improvement intervals clustered at training seed, with mask variability explicit.

The matrix already says C2 falsifies algebraic test-label reconstruction and duplicated seed/mask correlation; that is a strength. It still needs the above data-flow diagram / feature schema to make the rule executable and auditably leakage-free.

## Can the three mechanisms resolve D’s earlier problem?

**Partly, and materially.** Frozen probes distinguish “the representation contains linearly accessible task signal under this probe” from “the canonical decoder is poorly fit”; acute intervention distinguishes immediate computational displacement from retraining; adaptation quantifies recovery after rewiring. Together they give sharper explanations for a small behavioral gap than competence alone.

**Not yet completely.** The three assays are not automatically orthogonal. A frozen ridge probe is itself a trained readout; an “acute intervention” evaluated with a probe-fitted W and with the canonical W produces different estimands. The design must name which readout is held fixed for acute intervention and how each topology’s probe is fitted. Adaptation must say whether it trains only the decoder, recurrent network, or both, from what initialization, on which data, and for how many optimizer updates. Otherwise an observed contrast cannot be assigned to “readout,” “graph,” or “adaptation.”

A minimal interpretable layout is:

| Assay | Graph | Recurrent parameters | Readout | What it identifies |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Canonical | bio / null | jointly trained under declared initialization | canonical trained decoder | Published-style joint optimization behavior |
| Frozen-feature probe | bio / null | frozen at a declared checkpoint | separate train-only ridge per feature set | Linear task accessibility of each feature set |
| Acute intervention | bio model then endpoint intervention | identical values frozen | same bio-trained readout | Immediate effect of the graph change under a fixed operator readout |
| Post-intervention adaptation | null after intervention | trainable under declared equal budget | specify fixed vs trainable decoder | Recovery/adaptation capacity; not acute causal effect |

Keep these outputs and claims separate. This resolves the original conceptual conflation only after the implementation matches the table.

## Guidance-planning gate

**Current status: unresolved startup.** Mechanism v2 removes “competence ⇒ topology sensitivity” as the central causal claim and provides a potentially discriminating assay design. It does not yet show that the task projection predicts held-out outcomes, that the predictor is test-label-free in implementation, or that the effect is novel. Numeric-lock deferral is appropriate, but the contract itself states the practical margin, competent-horizon feasibility and natural-workload access remain unresolved. The finite compute budget is a hard ceiling, not evidence of adequate precision.

Before entering full f-research guidance planning, require:

1. focused literature screen and reviewer pass on exact remaining novelty;
2. leakage-proof predictor formula and split/estimand map;
3. S0 measured resource feasibility, including the complete assay cost;
4. a defensible practical-resolution margin and a feasible confirmation design within the measured compute cap, or an explicit decision to scope the paper more narrowly and revisit venue fit.

A narrowly scoped planning note for the above gates is reasonable. Do not label startup passed, issue a research guidance contract, authorize the full pilot/confirmation campaign, or infer any scientific result yet.
