## Scientific justification and limits

A bounded development-feasibility protocol is scientifically justified because the prospective design makes falsifiable comparisons, the independent design review found its finite controls and inference contract coherent, and the current local checks demonstrate that the principal frozen model and endpoint operations can run on MPS. The protocol can resolve concrete implementation risks within the user's explicit cap. The judgment is limited to spending that cap to establish feasibility and produce gate artifacts; it is not evidence that the mechanism works or that the four-hour ceiling will suffice. Stop on cap exhaustion or any preregistered support, balance, compatibility, or runtime failure; do not relax thresholds, add response candidates, or promote engineering success into a scientific result.

No scientific experiment, paid call, or paper score is reported by this review.
