# Candidate E paired-edge-set construction proposal

Owner: ccf-idea-optimizer → ccf-experiment-designer. Unaccepted design supplement; no graph response or outcome has been computed. Motivation: separately sampling masks and then balancing changed active mass introduces avoidable selection differences. A stricter candidate uses the same edited original edge identities in every arm.

## Construction

1. Partition non-self-loop edges by complete source/target type and sign/count/strength shared-group identity. Draw a deterministic, response-independent random order within each group. Form disjoint blocks of eight edges with distinct source and target endpoints. Blocks never overlap; unusable remainders are recorded. A source may occur in different groups/blocks, but no edge is edited twice.
2. For each block enumerate its 28 unordered edge pairs. A legal pair has distinct endpoints, creates no self-loop, and adds neither an original existing edge nor a duplicate candidate edge. The latter requires a whole-mask check even for individually valid pairs. A perfect matching in this eight-edge compatibility graph defines four disjoint target swaps. Every original edge in the block is changed exactly once, and the original target multiset is retained.
3. Enumerate the at most 105 perfect matchings of eight items; reject those with illegal pairs. Do not use response outcomes to decide whether a block enters the common structural support. Retain a block only if it supplies enough distinct legal matchings for the predeclared arms and a common geometric stratum; otherwise record the structural rejection. An unchanged candidate graph is never counted as an intervention.
4. Compute geometry signatures of **complete block matchings** from endpoint-derived source separation, changed-edge length/direction and boundary-position bins. Use a fixed graph-only binning. Restrict all arms to the same geometry-signature stratum of each block, selected independently of activity. If exact strata leave fewer than two distinct complete matchings, this strict version is infeasible; a coarser-balance version requires a versioned pre-pilot design decision and independent review, not a confirmation-time relaxation.
5. Within that common stratum choose: minimum full absolute-response source mismatch; uniform legal matching; minimum magnitude-only mismatch; and minimum mismatch using within-type/spatial-bin shuffled source vectors. The geometry arm uses the same edge sets and graph-only geometry criterion without responses. Ties and random-arm draws are predeclared and logged. Random arms may select the response-optimal matching; do not exclude that possibility to manufacture separation. Report coincident complete masks and effective ensemble diversity.
6. Compose block matchings, then verify the complete graph's constraints, exact degree, per-node signed/group strength, no parallel-edge creation, edge turnover and reciprocal-pair certificate. Reject a whole family uniformly if a mandatory topology certificate cannot be obtained under the fixed structural selection rule. Report the rejection rate and its conditioning; do not claim sampling from an unconditional structural-null distribution.

## Dose and interpretation

All arms edit exactly the same original edges, so original-edge count, original evoked active mass, parameter-group composition and source activity distribution match **exactly**, independent of differing pairings. Geometry is controlled by the common block stratum. This sharply tests vector correspondence versus magnitude and geometry. Outcome differences cannot be attributed to choosing quieter original edges. Aggregate forcing still must be measured after composition; pair scores do not imply a global trajectory bound.

Order retained blocks with a graph-only RNG. The development evoked-mass sweep selects a shared prefix approximating each target dose; record realized dose and indivisible-block error. A dose is eligible only if its error is at most one block's mass and all arms meet the same error rule. Never optimize different arm-specific prefixes on functional outcomes. Receiving-checkpoint dose for transferred masks is reported without reselection.

This stricter construction can fail: exact geometry strata may offer only one matching, sparse legal compatibility may prevent perfect matchings, topology certificates may be rare, or response/magnitude/random choices may coincide. These are informative feasibility failures, not grounds to silently weaken control balance. Static graph analysis can decide structural candidate diversity; activity contrast and function transfer remain development-pilot questions.

## Candidate-search accounting

For eight-edge blocks, 28 pair scores imply 3.5 pairs per edited edge. At 20% of 1,513,231 edges, the worst-case unique pair count is about 1.06 million, before structural rejection. Therefore the earlier proposed 200,000-pair cap cannot cover the full dose grid under this constructor. This supplement proposes a finite maximum of 1.1 million **unique scored pairs shared by all arms**, with rejected structural proposals separately counted and bounded. It does not authorize extra GPU hours: the pilot remains at most four cumulative GPU-hours. Before adoption, measured feature/scoring cost must fit that envelope and an independent review must accept the budget correction. No pilot budget has been spent on this constructor.

## Adoption gate

This is a candidate implementation refinement, not a final accepted mask generator. First audit static legal-matching and geometry-stratum diversity. If insufficient, retain the failure and compare a precisely balanced global construction before guidance acceptance. Do not claim actual common activity support or a positive functional effect from combinatorics alone.
