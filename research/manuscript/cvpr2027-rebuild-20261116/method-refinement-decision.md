# Method-refinement decision

The new `revision-method-refinement/` replaces `revision-cvpr26-lift/` as the best current manuscript. Its independent full review is Overall 6.0 with confidence 5, compared with 5.5 for the locked draft. The gain comes from clearer science, not new evidence.

## What changed

- The Task-Information Audit is now an explicit four-input, four-step protocol with three output levels: structural constraints, task information at the readout, and finite-model behavior.
- The executed transfer is derived from all two-step source--relay--sink paths, showing why common-relay and module counts do not determine readout inputs.
- Source visibility is separated from source recovery; the ideal XOR proposition is kept as a task-specific population result. A private transfer residual extends the same analysis to simultaneous visual sources.
- Figure 1 directly shows equal structural certificates with different reconstructed transfers and clearly separated archived accuracies. Figure 3 marks ideal Bayes status beside finite dose curves. Figure 4 connects private transfer geometry to source-specific CIFAR readouts.
- Experiments are organized by two questions: whether structural matching preserves information, and whether information preservation predicts finite use. The abstract, introduction, and discussion use the same three-level vocabulary.
- A repeated summary table was removed to keep the scientific content within eight pages.

## Why this version

The methodological framing is now specific enough to be executable for the studied fixed linear operator, and the new figures make the central argument checkable. The independent reviewer judged soundness and clarity improved while noting that novelty, evidence breadth, and CVPR significance remain limited.

## Remaining risks, ordered by severity

1. The evidence covers one male connectome, related extractions, one propagation rule and a synthetic task; it does not validate the audit across operators or independent biological samples.
2. Central historical outcomes are consistency evidence because original dose-instance hashes and bit-identical initializations are unavailable.
3. The CIFAR-10 assay is a four-image readout probe, not a consequential natural-image recognition result; private transfer residual is an operator-level descriptor, not a general decoding necessity condition.
4. The underlying algebra remains simple, so the contribution rests on the audit protocol and counterexample rather than a new algorithm or architecture.

No new training, data expansion, or experiment was performed. All newly drawn quantities are deterministic projections or replots of archived records.
